# Blockers and change requests

When a Builder cannot safely complete a specification without an unapproved decision, invariant change, forbidden path, live authority, or scope expansion, append:

- Date and specification ID
- What is blocked
- Why it cannot be resolved within the approved specification
- What was tried
- Evidence and repository references
- Recommended owner/Architect decision

Then stop. A blocker is an honest control outcome, not a failure to appear autonomous.
